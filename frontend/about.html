About FoodConnect

FoodConnect is a technology-driven platform designed to address the critical issue of food waste and food insecurity. Every year, millions of tons of edible food are discarded while millions of people struggle to access nutritious meals.

This system creates a structured connection between food donors, volunteers, and administrative authorities to ensure safe, efficient, and transparent redistribution of surplus food.

Our Mission

To minimize food waste by enabling real-time coordination between donors and volunteers, ensuring that surplus food reaches underprivileged communities efficiently and responsibly.

Our Vision

To build a sustainable and socially responsible ecosystem where no edible food is wasted and every individual has access to basic nutritional needs.

Key Objectives

• Reduce large-scale food wastage.

• Support NGOs, ashrams, and community shelters.

• Encourage volunteer participation and social responsibility.

• Promote environmentally sustainable food management.

How the System Works

1. Users register and submit details of surplus food along with location.

2. Admin verifies and approves the request.

3. Approved volunteers are notified to collect and distribute food.

4. Food is delivered to NGOs, ashrams, or community centers.

Building a responsible and sustainable food sharing ecosystem.
